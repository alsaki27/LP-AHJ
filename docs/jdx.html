How to read this page. Each authority was investigated separately from its own sources (permit pages, application forms, codes, resolutions, staff pages), checked 2026-11-24. A timeline is shown only when we can point to a source: Published means the agency states it; Reported means a credible secondary source; Call to verify means nothing is published, so we say who to call and exactly what to ask instead of guessing. Any "planning allowance" is our own assumption and is labelled as such. Where agencies disagree with each other, the conflict is shown.
