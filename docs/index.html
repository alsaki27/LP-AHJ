How we built the record

  1. Measured the original routes. Used 52 KMZ features to measure route length and crossing stations. Screened road control, parcels and available right-of-way records.
  2. Recomputed the crossings. Intersected the routes with FRA/NARN rail, ODOT stream and Illinois EPA flowlines. Checked structures, aerials, wetlands and available FEMA flood mapping.
  3. Compared construction and permit options. Used owner records and agency requirements to identify blockers, approval steps, submittals and fallbacks for each mapped water point.

Mapped points and candidate authorities are design screening. Field survey, recorded rights and agency responses establish final work limits and approvals.
