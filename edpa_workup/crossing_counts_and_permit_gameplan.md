The water register has **16 entries total**: 7 exact GIS centerline intersections plus 9 unresolved or non-exact candidates. ## Cross-cutting permit and construction sequence

1. **Lock geometry:** survey the proposed facility, highway ROW/access-control, structure limits, railroad valuation boundaries, utilities, property/easements, channel banks and wetlands. Resolve each exact vs. near screen and avoid double-counting adjacent reaches or arcs.
2. **Select crossing method:** evaluate HDD beneath channel and scour; pits outside waters/wetlands/floodway; bridge attachment only after owner/as-built approval; existing casing only after capacity, station match, title, and written rights are confirmed. Add frac-out containment and stop-work plan for HDD near water.
3. **Pre-application:** present one complete GIS/map, plan/profile, impact table, construction access/staging, schedule and restoration concept to the responsible USACE district and state agency. Ask for written NWP/PCN/individual-permit and 401 path; do not infer a permit from an NWI polygon or bridge label.
4. **Parallel road/rail/property approvals:** open CSX Property Portal, NS RailPros, or UP/CJR cases at concept stage; simultaneously confirm road authority, bridge owner, property owner, access-control, traffic, signal/utility locate and flagging. Railroad agreements and public permits are separate.
5. **Schedule gates:** do not release construction until executed railroad/property agreements, permits, insurance, road occupancy/traffic approvals, flagging/monitoring slots, utility locates and land access are in hand. Treat published portal clocks as administrative targets, not total end-to-end construction-ready durations.
