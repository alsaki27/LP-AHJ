# Railroad and waterway inventory by route

**Prepared:** 23 November 2026  
**Purpose:** One place to see the rail corridors and named, unnamed, or suspected water features recorded for each of the three project route groups. This is a screening inventory compiled from the project GIS/registers and the November 2026 NAIP image review. It is not a field-verified crossing count, title determination, jurisdictional delineation, or permit decision.

## How to read this inventory

- **Route groups:** Chicago spurs, Marysville–New Albany Segment 0, and Marysville–New Albany Segment 1.
- **Exact / intersecting** means the source route geometry intersects a mapped rail line or mapped water centerline in the cited screening layer. It does not prove a physical crossing, ownership, channel limits, railroad ROW, or permit trigger.
- **Near / candidate** means a GIS feature or ODOT structure is close to the route, or the route intersects a wetland/streambed screening polygon without a verified channel intersection. These are not added as confirmed crossings.
- **Station** is measured along the individual source KMZ feature from its start, in feet, unless identified as a route-line station. It is not mileage from the project endpoint.
- Repeated names and structure records may refer to the same waterway, adjacent structures, or a nearby reach. Do not sum rows as unique waterways without geometry and field reconciliation.
