## Executive summary

Segment 1 contains 19 source features and about **122,977 ft (23.29 mi)** of geodesic linework. Its source labels—`Bore`, `Water`, `HW`, and `RR`—are unverified. The KMZ sequence runs toward Marysville, opposite the RFP’s “Marysville to New Albany” wording, and Segment 0 ends about **31.1 m from Segment 1**. Confirm route direction, terminal facilities, gap treatment, fiber/conduit design basis and billable-footage basis before committing quantity or continuous delivery dates.

The segment is geographically in **Delaware County for ~63,317 ft (11.99 mi)** and **Union County for ~59,666 ft (11.30 mi)**. The county line falls inside S1-S036 at feature station **6,733.6 ft**. Powell and Marysville city limits create additional permit/fee gates. County, city and township boundaries are screening overlays; they do not establish road ownership or cable ROW.

The parcel screen provides actionable property leads. Within a common 250-ft corridor, the segment links to **515 uniquely keyed tax parcels plus one generic assessor ROW polygon**. **188 uniquely keyed tax parcels directly intersect** the route; the ROW polygon `999999-0` also overlaps near the S0/S1 seam but is not a unique fee parcel. The export contains 1,249 feature-linked source rows because parcels span multiple features. A direct parcel overlay is not a deeded easement or permission to enter. Assessor-owner values are reported as assessor leads; title is not confirmed.

Two P0 constraints affect price and schedule:

1. **S1-S032–S1-S034 / O’Shaughnessy Reservoir and Scioto:** Delaware County assessor owner fields identify City of Columbus parcels whose legal descriptions reference the reservoir/watershed and riverbed. The parcel layer directly overlaps the final **220.3 ft** of S1-S032, **all 1,602.8 ft** of S1-S033 and the first **667.5 ft** of S1-S034. City records say City-owned reservoir shoreland protects drinking-water supply and that access/modification of City land requires City authorization. The published Land Stewardship License is described for adjacent landowners; it is not evidence that this utility project qualifies for that license or has an approved utility access path. Secure title/easement and written City access/license direction before survey, test holes, staging or construction. These assessor parcels lie outside Columbus corporate limits, so City land ownership does not by itself create a Columbus street excavation permit. [Columbus reservoir FAQ](https://www.columbus.gov/Services/Columbus-Water-Power/About-Columbus-Water-Power/The-Division-of-Water/Reservoir-Land-Stewardship-Program/FAQs).
2. **S1-S031 and S1-S045 rail crossings:** CSX/NARN track geometry intersects both lines. S1-S031 also overlaps assessor parcel `319240-0` labeled Railroad Right of Way; S1-S045 overlaps parcel `1020100900000-2900022300010` whose assessor owner field is New York Central Lines LLC. **CSX/CSXT is the operating and permitting path**; the exact current fee owner and contract signatory remain to be confirmed from CSX title/valuation/lease records. CSX’s current packet reports an average ~30-day review; HDD is a variance and can add review fees, monitoring and schedule. [CSX Utility Permits](https://www.csx.com/index.cfm/customers/value-added-services/property-real-estate/permitting-utility-wireless-infrastructure-installations-and-rights-of-entry/utility-permits/), [CSX Information Packet v4.0, February 2026](https://www.csx.com/share/wwwcsx15/assets/File/Customers/Property/permit-information-packet.pdf).

## 8. Existing utilities and permit-ready design

Ohio Revised Code 3781.27 (amended **effective June 9, 2026**) is the route-wide design baseline. A designer for excavation must submit an Ohio811 design notification to determine utility names, types and tolerance zones from current utility records. Participating utilities must respond within **10 working days**, or by a later date agreed with the designer; the designer must show approximate utility locations, utility names/addresses/phone numbers, required adjustments and utility adjustment time on/with the plans, design to avoid conflict, and attempt **at least 12-in clearance**. Less than 12 in requires existing-utility owners to be notified in writing before installation. This design notice and plans are distinct from the later construction locate/mark ticket. [ORC 3781.27, effective June 9, 2026](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-3781.27).
