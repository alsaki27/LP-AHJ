## Director’s read

This image review did not establish a new, confirmed railroad or regulated-water crossing omitted from the existing registers. It did identify specific geometry, structure, and field checks that should remain open before the route counts, cost, or permit schedule are treated as firm:

1. **Home Road / O’Shaughnessy Reservoir (Segment 1, S1-S033, ODOT SFN 2134462) — critical design decision.** The image shows Home Road on a long bridge/causeway across the reservoir, with the SR 257 roundabout and boat-ramp/marina context at the east approach. Decide whether the concept is a bridge attachment, an approach/roadside alignment, or a route around the reservoir. Bridge ownership, structure limits, attachment rights, dam/water-control interfaces, and permits are still unconfirmed.
2. **CSX location QA (Chicago Spurs T10/T13) — resolve before rail counts.** The GIS register identifies exact rail intersections, but the image pins do not visually align cleanly with the strongest track corridors. Reconcile the route KMZ, target coordinates, FRA/NARN rail geometry, crossing IDs, and current railroad property/track plans. Do not remove or reclassify these crossings based on imagery alone.
3. **Potential rail adjacency (Segment 0 S0-S021) — property overlay needed.** A long route feature continues beside a substantial industrial rail/track-storage environment near the registered S0-S020 CSX Busch Lead. The image cannot tell whether it enters railroad property or approaches another track. Add the full feature to the railroad valuation-map and title overlay.
4. **Segment 1 drainage-candidate completeness — retain the 14 structure leads.** The crops generally support wooded drainage, lowland, pond, or road-crossing settings for 14 ODOT structure/ditch candidates not represented as separate named events in the six-row water register. They are candidate checks, not 14 confirmed regulated-water crossings. Reconcile each with the route line, ODOT structure plans, field survey, and applicable water/wetland screening.
5. **Quarry / Watkins Road (Segment 1, SFN 8031045) — major access and operations issue.** Active quarry operations, heavy access, excavations, and pond/settling features appear beside the road target. This is a parcel, safety, haul-route, staging, and drainage coordination blocker. The imagery does not prove the route crosses a quarry pond or regulated water.
6. **Salt Creek bridge interface (Chicago Spur1) — preserve the bridge-vs-bore decision.** A broad wooded floodplain and existing road bridge are visible. No utility attachment is discernible. Obtain bridge plans and owner criteria, survey the bridge and approaches, and carry an HDD alternative until a bridge attachment is accepted.
7. **Spur4 drainage lead (Chicago) and Segment 0 S0-S006 — unresolved water features.** Spur4 has a narrow vegetated corridor consistent with a possible ditch, swale, or culvert, but no open water is apparent. S0-S006 remains unresolved near Gruber Run. Preserve both for survey and drainage-owner verification.
8. **ODOT highway and interchange interfaces — work packages remain.** Segment 0 S0-S014/S0-S018/S0-S022 and Segment 1 US 33/US 36 (SFNs 8001170/8001200) show extensive limited-access roadway, ramp, bridge, and traffic-control environments. Obtain current ROW/access-control, utility, structure, and project plans before selecting a bore or attachment method.

## Review method, provenance, and limits

The snapshot atlas was generated from source-register coordinates with a red target crosshair and coordinate/feature label. Coverage consists of discrete crops; the target is a screening location, not a surveyed crossing coordinate, and the proposed cable centerline is not drawn over the pixels. Segment 0 includes a corridor sample set with no more than approximately 5,000 ft spacing along longer route features. The review is suitable for finding visible context and prioritizing follow-up, not for ruling out features between targets.

The images are from the public USGS/USDA FSA National Agriculture Imagery Program service, with service attribution. The displayed `2025-01-09` copyright/metadata value is not a confirmed per-pixel acquisition date. Google Maps satellite URLs are provided as live references only; no Google Earth/Maps screenshots are included. Sources: [USGS NAIP ImageServer](https://imagery.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USGSNAIPImagery/ImageServer), [USDA FSA NAIP rights notice](https://www.fsa.usda.gov/Internet/FSA_Notice/ap_26.pdf), [Google Geo usage guidance](https://about.google/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/geo-guidelines/).

Aerial imagery cannot establish legal ownership, railroad operator, public right-of-way, utility rights, waterbody or wetland boundaries, Section 404/10/401 jurisdiction, navigability, bridge condition, attachment rights, track count, construction status, or permit applicability. Tree canopy, image date, resolution, and coordinate alignment can conceal or misplace apparent features. All records and recommendations here remain screening leads requiring current authoritative records, survey, field review, and agency/owner confirmation.
