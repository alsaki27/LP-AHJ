# Segment 0 — NAIP Visual Gap Review

**Scope:** Review of all 30 sample crops indexed for Segment 0, covering the 27 source-KMZ features (about 27.97 mi). The crops are point-centered and cover roughly 900 × 650 m each; long features have multiple samples. Review date: 23 November 2026. The imagery is the public USGS/USDA National Agriculture Imagery Program (NAIP) service. The index shows `2025-01-09` as service copyright metadata, **not a verified image acquisition date**. The individual screenshot footer records retrieval on 23 November 2026 and labels them visual screening only.

## Executive readout

The imagery **does not establish a new, confirmed railroad or water crossing missing from the existing Segment 0 registers**. It visually supports several already-identified stream/rail/highway candidates and adds one material follow-up: the road segment immediately north of the mapped CSX lead at **S0-S021** sits beside an extensive industrial rail/track-storage environment. The current railroad register has a S0-S020 CSX candidate but no separate S0-S021 rail record. Check the full S0-S021 alignment against current track centerlines, valuation/property maps, switches and parcel boundaries before ruling out an additional rail encroachment or lead.

The crops also reveal several development, detention-basin and drainage interfaces along bore-labeled features. Those are constructability and field-verification flags, not evidence of a jurisdictional water crossing. The 13-feature NWI screen remains the right basis for wetland follow-up; imagery cannot replace delineation or jurisdictional review.

## Coverage and limits

- Every supplied Segment 0 source feature has at least one crop; the longer source lines have additional sample points, with spacing no greater than about 5,000 ft along each line. Crops are still discrete windows, not a continuous orthophoto strip; a short crossing between or outside the crops could be missed.
- The snapshots do not show the proposed centerline overlay. Register IDs and coordinates are the link between the images and the route, not a surveyed tie.
- The service metadata date is not a per-pixel acquisition date. Construction, vegetation, road works and track usage may have changed since acquisition.
- NAIP appearance cannot establish railroad ownership/operator, fee title, legal ROW, utility rights, waterbody name or navigability, wetland boundary, Section 404/10/401 jurisdiction, floodway, bridge attachment permission, or current work status. No visual candidate should be treated as agency concurrence or permit determination.
- The detailed row-by-row observations, image paths, map links, register comparison, confidence and field actions are in [`segment0_naip_visual_findings.csv`](segment0_naip_visual_findings.csv). The project-wide source/metadata/index is [`../naip_snapshot_index.csv`](../naip_snapshot_index.csv).

## Bottom line

No new crossing can be called confirmed from these images. Add **S0-S021 rail adjacency/ownership verification** as the principal potential register gap, and carry the visible detention basins, grading and wet-ground corridors as construction coordination and field-check items alongside the existing crossing list.
