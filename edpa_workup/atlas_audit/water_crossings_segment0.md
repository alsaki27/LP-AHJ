## Bid and permit sequence

**Separate the five major creek/river decisions** at South Fork, Blacklick, Rocky Fork bridge area, Big Walnut and Alum from routine culvert clearance. Treat S008 as a distinct stream location pending identification. Confirm the owner and approved method before combining costs or committing to attachment. Carry ODOT D6 review where state ROW or a state asset is implicated. Treat a state-route number and a county permit lead as separate cues until a recorded plan ties work and permit issuer together. [Ohio Revised Code §5515.01](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-5515.01) · [ODOT bridge guidance, REM 8108 and BDM](https://www.transportation.ohio.gov/business/publications/aesthetic-design-guidelines)

**Four points returned a mapped floodway:** Blacklick, S010, Big Walnut and Alum. Provide the local floodplain administrator a plan showing work limits, construction access, placement of temporary fill, equipment and changes to grade. Ask what hydraulic/no-rise documentation applies. Provide Huntington and Ohio EPA a single route cover sheet plus site-level plans, delineation and temporary/permanent impact tables. Apply Section 10, Section 404, NWP 57, §401 and isolated wetland screens to the actual work. The [overall permitting playbook](water_crossing_deep_dive.md) sets out the agency process. [FEMA flood hazard data](https://www.fema.gov/flood-maps/national-flood-hazard-layer) · [Ohio EPA](https://epa.ohio.gov/divisions-and-offices/surface-water/permitting/water-quality-certification-and-isolated-wetland-permits)

Both have a possible NS property interface on arc 418770 and A adds the East Orange CSX/NS group. Price avoided state occupation separately from these introduced duties.
