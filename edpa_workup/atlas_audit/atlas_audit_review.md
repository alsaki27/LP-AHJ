## Claims supported by the checks

The **52 base lines** match every original KMZ vertex at the atlas’s five-decimal precision. Using the atlas’s UTM projections and rounding each line gives **147,653 ft** for Segment 0, **122,981 ft** for Segment 1 and **49,139 ft** for Chicago. The full raw projected lengths are 147,653.40, 122,980.08 and 49,138.92 ft respectively. The display rounds at feature level, so retain method and precision when comparing quantities from different GIS exports.

The **11 rail points** reproduce against live NARN lines, including two intersections on CJR arc 507532. The layer identifies **CJR (6), CSXT (4), and NS (1)**. The six Chicago FRA road crossing IDs are nearby associated road records. The `FRA crossing ID (nearest)` column in the report table is blank even where the Chicago risk cards have these IDs. NARN marks also have a different purpose from a property instrument. UP identifies CJR as the operator and tenant at Centex. [FRA attribute definitions](https://railroads.dot.gov/sites/fra.dot.gov/files/2025-03/North%20American%20Rail%20Network%20-%20Lines%20Attribute%20List.pdf) · [UP Chicago Junction profile](https://www.up.com/customers/shortline/lines/ctm/)

All **85 imagery target keys** trace to the LP-AHJ NAIP index and all listed JPEGs exist in the ZIP. A matching crop validates that an observation can be traced. The NAIP acquisition date and actual surveyed work limits remain open.

## Structural issues that affect multiple records

- **The map pins for construction are approximate positions on proposed route features.** The builder assigns the first listed feature’s midpoint to every project marker. It does not use the project station, road intersection or official project footprint. Segment 1 QA measured a 5,710-ft error against the midpoint of the displayed S042 station range on SEG1U-01. Five cards with empty feature lists get no map marker. Evidence is in `build_data.py` lines 603–624 and the embedded `construction` records.
- **The “45 projects” KPI counts cards.** The array contains 45 records, but three are categorized as moratoriums. At least two project families are represented in more than one route area, Home Road rail grade separation and Home Road Water Plant. The current count describes entries in the app. A unique construction project count needs deduplication and consistent treatment of packages such as plant and transmission mains. Evidence is in `construction.html` lines 40–42 and records SEG0C-05, SPURS-09, SPURS-10, SEG0DEL-04/SEG1D-05 and SEG0DEL-05/SEG1D-01.
- **The total station classifications have small residuals.** The 399 road authority runs and 355 land runs each sum to 319,645 ft against 319,773 ft for the 52 base lines. The differences are 68 ft in S0, 46 ft in S1 and 14 ft in Chicago. This is consistent with sampled station rounding. In the 41 DOT gates, some start/end stations describe the outer envelope for several intervals. The Chicago Arlington Heights 7,430–9,230 ft envelope has 1,600 ft of road exposure after a 200-ft Bennett segment is excluded. Show the two Arlington Heights intervals.
- **ROW widths are parcel-map spacing.** The app says the widths are measured from polygon gaps at sampled points and capped at 175 ft. Its grouping combines mapped and presumptive ROW. The underlying station samples, remapping module, parcel-to-road inference, recorded instruments and exact parcel coverage are needed to reproduce the 33 road width rows, all 399 issuer assignments and all 355 land classifications. A county/municipal boundary does not establish ROW, maintenance or permit authority.
- **The source package cannot regenerate its own numbers.** The ZIP has the browser bundle and builder. The builder requires an external baseline XLSX, `geom.json`, `parcel_samples2.json`, `remap.py`, jurisdiction JSON, project JSON and opportunity source files under local Claude paths. Those inputs are absent from the archive. This specifically limits evidence for aggregated boundary, parcel, fee/contact status, clear-corridor, and schedule claims. The audit can reproduce the base alignment and intersect the primary layers independently.

## Audit coverage and remaining evidence

The three route reviews trace the individual risk, permit and construction records to public evidence where available. The shipped data inventory includes 52 base lines, 399 authority runs, 355 land runs, 6 county and 11 place shapes, 36 risks, 23 leads, 46 jurisdiction cards, 11 reroutes, 45 construction cards, 49 “checked clear” notes, 35 gaps, 85 images and 12 open items. Report tables also include 146 boundary entries, 32 parcel-sheet rows, 14 road-maintenance rows, 41 DOT gates, 12 rail rows including a note, and 33 ROW-width rows. The included workbook summary has 58 rows and the builder populates a second summary field with zero rows.

I recomputed the base alignments, entire-corridor rail and water GIS screens, run totals, and reroute dimensions from files and live services. The agency returned 160 and 67 ODOT stream lines for the Ohio route envelopes and 17 Illinois EPA lines for the Chicago envelope. The NARN response held 32, 19 and 30 lines for S0, S1 and Chicago respectively. No response indicated truncation. I intersected the official polylines against the original KMZ lines in UTM 17N for Ohio and UTM 16N for Illinois. Station is projected feet along the source feature. The saved service responses are in `.atlas_audit_input`. Screening is bounded by the source layers and their mapped feature dates.

The segment reports distinguish source-supported statements, contradictions and unresolved claims. Individual field verification remains open where a build input, controlling deed, agency approval, survey, operating rights, sealed plans, exact project footprint or current owner quote is missing. The 49 negative construction searches are date- and source-bound, so each remains an open evidence status unless the authority directly confirms it.

**Scope of changes:** This audit created separate reports and evidence. The original ZIP and the webapp were not edited or pushed.
