# Chicago spurs | Water crossing engineering review

Three mapped flowline points. The IDOT Structures inventory now ties two of them to individual road structures. Survey station is measured in feet from the start of the original KMZ feature. Parcel, wetland and flood mapping evidence for each point is in the [crossing evidence table](water_crossing_point_evidence.csv).

## Spur1-S01 at 1,615.4 ft | Devon Avenue and Arlington Heights Road

**Evidence.** Illinois EPA intermittent line 109975692 intersects the proposed route at 41.99238888, −88.00291791, at about 72° to the local mapped stream line. Near the Devon/Arlington Heights transition, the atlas assigns this sample to Devon and DuPage DOT. The point falls in Itasca, DuPage County. Prior title screening also found deeded Arlington Heights road polygons in this station area referencing document 507662. Resolve the road-leg interface before allocating permit frontage. The FEMA point returned Zone X and NWI mapped a pond within 100 m. The IDOT Structures query returned no inventory point in the small search envelope. An unlisted local pipe or buried storm connection remains plausible. Nearby tax PINs include 0305201059 and 0305201060.

**Carry a short trenchless crossing allowance.** Have DuPage DOT and Itasca provide Devon and intersection drainage/as-built plans. Walk the drainage to both sides, locate inverts and determine whether the mapped line is a pipe, ditch or older alignment. If culverted, place a separate bore around the pipe’s foundation and inlet/outlet envelope, with pits in verified upland ROW. If open, design below the bed and scour with restored approaches. A small alignment shift toward a surveyed road crossing is the alternate. A shift to the opposite side of the intersection can change agency control and add road crossings, so compare before committing.

**Close the decision.** Ask DuPage Highway Permits and Itasca Engineering for a station-tied controller and drainage-owner decision with the deed and a plan/profile. Add Chicago District, IDNR/OWR and Illinois EPA review if the footprint triggers it. Escalate out of the routine bore allowance if pits intersect the pond, its outlet, wetlands, or private land. [DuPage permits](https://www.dupagecounty.gov/government/departments/transportation/doing_business/permits/construction_permits.php) · [Itasca utility plan requirements](https://www.itasca.com/DocumentCenter/View/16622/Utility-Permit-projects-Requirmentspdf)

## Spur1-S02 at 197.8 ft | Salt Creek at Arlington Heights Road

**Evidence.** Illinois EPA line 109975638 crosses at about 87°. A new search identified **IDOT structure 016-3014**, OID 7553, Arlington Heights Road over Salt Creek. The inventory lists 1971 construction, 62 ft overall structure length, 57.5 ft long span and 74 ft deck width. These establish an identifiable structure and a reasonable owner-record request. They do not establish water width, spare duct or allowable attachment load. The NAIP crop shows the bridge in a broader vegetated corridor. NWI returns riverine polygons R2UBHx and R5UBH. This pass did not get a FEMA point response. Obtain the effective FIRM and floodway before laying out either method. The Meacham Road bridge over the West Branch is a different structure. [IDOT Structures layer](https://gis1.dot.illinois.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TrafficSafety/cis_LocationTool2026/MapServer/4) · [Cook County utility intake](https://www.cookcountyil.gov/service/constructionmaintenance-permits)

| Option | What makes it viable | What can kill it |
|---|---|---|
| **HDD in the Arlington Heights corridor — base** | A near-normal bore lets both pits sit outside the surveyed banks, wetlands and floodway if road and property limits provide room. Get road and structure as-builts, surveyed profiles, utility elevations, geotechnical conditions, scour depth and hydraulic limits. Set the bore below the controlling bed/scour and foundation envelope, then build the drilling fluid monitoring and response plan. | No lawful rig/pit access, insufficient clearance to abutments or buried utilities, fluid return risk in permeable soils, or approach disturbance that exceeds the permit basis. Test a small side shift before adopting a different crossing road. |
| **Existing licensed duct or structural attachment — alternate** | Cook DoTH confirms structure control and provides as-builts, a recent inspection, utility records and an acceptable attachment zone. Check spare duct first. A new conduit needs a sealed load and connection design, clearance and access drawings, traffic plan and approach transitions. | No capacity or rights, an incompatible rehabilitation plan, unacceptable access under the bridge, or a design that affects the flood opening or structure maintenance. A field photo of a pipe has no bearing on spare rights. |
| **Local bypass** | An alternate ROW has room for pits and a surveyed crossing with simpler access. Require equivalent endpoints and a fresh parcel/road/water overlay. | Crossing the same creek on a different road can add another long approach, utility conflicts and another jurisdiction. Meacham’s bridge project is independent and still has its own approval and access requirements. |

**Permit sequence.** Cook DoTH permit/structures review comes first in parallel with a survey of the effective floodway, riparian areas and wetlands. Have Elk Grove and MWRD determine whether the proposed work meets the restored utility ROW exemption or disturbs a Flood Protection Area. Ask IDNR/OWR whether Statewide Permit 8 or Regional Permit 3 covers the actual HDD footprint and whether a local approval is required. SP8’s applicability text separates directional boring from the exclusions applied to other crossings. For federal review, take the work limits to USACE Chicago and ask whether §404 or §10 applies at this reach. Illinois EPA’s 2026 certification has linear-project disturbance/length triggers and a Salt Creek sidecasting provision. If a federal permit that may result in a discharge is needed, document the general or individual §401 path. Size staging and spoil control around that result. [IDNR SP8](https://dnr.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/dnr/waterresources/documents/resmanstatewidepermit8.pdf) · [IDNR RP3](https://dnr.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/dnr/waterresources/documents/resmanregionalpermit3.pdf) · [MWRD permit determination](https://www.mwrd.org/doing-business/watershed-management-ordinance-and-infiltrationinflow/watershed-management-ordinance-wmo-and-technical-guidance-manual-tgm/watershed-management-ordinance-wmo-frequently-asked-questions-faqs) · [Illinois EPA 2026 certification](https://epa.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/epa/topics/water-quality/401-program/Illinois%20EPA%20401%20Water%20Quality%20Certification%20for%202026%20NWPs.pdf)

## Spur4-S01 at 7,891.3 ft | Willow Creek at Arthur Avenue

**Evidence.** The unnamed Illinois EPA intermittent line 109975676, the NWI R4SBC lead about 16 station feet away and **IDOT culvert 016-7861** refer to the same crossing area. IDOT calls the crossed feature **Willow Creek**, carried by Arthur Avenue. The inventory reports a 2005 structure, three cells, listed 6-ft culvert width and 5-ft height. Those dimensions describe the inventory fields. Confirm the overall structure footprint, barrel orientation, foundations and end treatments from plans. The point is about 89° to the mapped line. This pass did not get a FEMA point response. The older report’s “NWI only” status should be replaced by a mapped and structure supported crossing, with one work item pending survey. [IDOT Structures layer, OID 7192](https://gis1.dot.illinois.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TrafficSafety/cis_LocationTool2026/MapServer/4/7192)

**Carry a separate culvert-clearance bore.** Obtain Elk Grove’s culvert plans, parallel water/sewer elevations, pavement restoration limits and industrial driveway work windows. Survey the two headwalls, inlet/outlet and outer barrel/foundation limits. Develop an adjacent bore in verified ROW, or a deeper bore outside the owner’s foundation exclusion if there is no lateral path. Avoid using any of the hydraulic cells for conduit. If the first profile fails, shift locally across Arthur within verified public corridor, preserving truck access. Carry an easement allowance if pit or rig space extends onto any frontage parcel.

**Close the decision.** Start with Elk Grove Engineering for the culvert and work envelope. Check Cook or IDOT only if pits/access reach their facilities, including IL 83. Use the same MWRD, IDNR and USACE/IEPA screens if waters or flood protection areas are affected. Coordinate structure inspection and road timing before setting rig dates.
