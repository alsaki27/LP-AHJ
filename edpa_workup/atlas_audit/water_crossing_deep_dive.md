## How I would choose a method

| Method | Use where | Information required before pricing or committing |
|---|---|---|
| **Routine trenchless clearance bore** | Small roadside drainage or culvert with short, lawful, dry access on both sides. | Inlet/outlet, pipe and utilities, foundation/end treatment limits, roadside drainage grade, back of ROW, bore profile and pits. Shift across or along the road only after confirming new traffic and property effects. |
| **Engineered HDD** | Major open water, sensitive banks or no usable bridge pathway. | Topographic and bed survey, scour, geotechnical profile and groundwater, rig and pullback envelopes, utility elevations, bore stresses and length, ground loss/fluid pressure and return response, access/easements, drill water and spoil handling. Compare both approaches and route side shifts. |
| **Existing duct** | A continuous usable licensed duct crosses the obstacle. | Owner and permanent rights, inside diameter and spare position, transitions and physical continuity, existing occupants and proof of condition; inspections, access and pull length. |
| **New bridge attachment** | Existing bridge gives a shorter civil footprint with a permissible load and access envelope. | Structure owner approval, sealed attachment and load/connection check, recent inspection and rehab plans, navigation/freeboard, joint movement, protections and access, landfalls and recorded permission where owner rights differ. Temporary platforms, boats, spanning work and approach disturbance can affect the permit path. |
| **Local shift or alternate road** | Existing pit or attachment location is blocked by title, structure or geometry. | Compare the same endpoints, check new ROW and habitat, identify every introduced flowline/structure/rail interface and obtain road control and staging. Confirm whether the customer needs the original tie-ins or corridor for service. |

For each bid package carry the design survey and record request, build method, permit and property instrument, traffic/staging, restoration and inspection as identifiable cost items. A FEMA or structure offset does not supply a bore length. Quote bore construction using the approved work profile and bore entry/exit rather than the length of a KMZ feature or inventory span.

## Permit checks that can change the schedule

1. **Ask the USACE district about the exact reach, method and footprints.** For Ohio use Huntington as the starting regulatory office for the listed locations and check the district at intake. For Illinois use Chicago. The 2026 NWP 57 text includes work over, in or under Section 10 waters, including the discharge-free examples in the permit language. Huntington’s summary lists the Scioto and Olentangy. Carry a Section 10 review and PCN as the planning case at the two Home Road bridges pending exact reach, scope and district confirmation. Screen §404 separately for dredged/fill discharge, including staging mats, temporary fills, bank work and any work in jurisdictional wetlands. Federal NWP 57 has a half-acre loss cap per single and complete project and baseline PCN triggers for a §10 permit or discharge resulting in more than 0.1 ac loss. General and regional conditions may require notification or consultation sooner. [USACE Ohio regulatory hub](https://www.lrd.usace.army.mil/Missions/Regulatory/Ohio/) · [2026 NWP 57 text and GC 32](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-01-08/html/2026-00121.htm) · [USACE RRS](https://rrs.usace.army.mil/)
2. **Separate the federal clock and state certification.** GC 32 provides 30 calendar days to determine PCN completeness, and its general 45-calendar-day rule measures from receipt of a complete PCN, subject to written direction and exceptions such as endangered species, historic resources, waiver and individual permit. Begin written agency consultation while preparing plans, access and delineation. When a federal license or permit may result in discharge, check §401 coverage against the agency’s exact conditions. Ohio EPA’s eBusiness intake handles §401, Director’s Authorization and isolated wetlands as applicable. For Illinois, evaluate aggregate disturbance and project length under the state certification, and regional PCN conditions such as forested wetland clearing, parallel stream placement, Section 10 and Hine’s emerald dragonfly geographic screening. [Ohio EPA 2026 certification and intake](https://epa.ohio.gov/divisions-and-offices/surface-water/permitting/water-quality-certification-and-isolated-wetland-permits) · [Illinois EPA certification](https://epa.illinois.gov/topics/forms/water-permits/401-water-quality-certification.html) · [Illinois regional conditions](https://www.mvm.usace.army.mil/Portals/51/2026%20NWP%20Regional%20Conditions_IL.pdf)
3. **Run floodplain, road, bridge and property reviews in parallel.** FEMA Zones A/AE and the regulatory floodway affect the work footprint, including temporary staging. Have the local floodplain office identify permit and any hydraulic/no-rise requirements based on the plan. In Cook County, get a flood protection and utility exemption determination from the local authority/MWRD and IDNR/OWR after documenting the construction envelope. In Ohio, obtain the bridge owner’s written terms, road permit, applicable state property instrument and private or public-land access. [MWRD permit types and published response targets](https://www.mwrd.org/doing-business/watershed-management-ordinance-and-infiltrationinflow/watershed-management-ordinance-wmo-and-technical-guidance-manual-tgm/watershed-management-ordinance-wmo-frequently-asked-questions-faqs) · [IDNR permit pathways](https://dnr.illinois.gov/waterresources/permitprograms.html) · [ODOT bridge utility policy](https://www.transportation.ohio.gov/business/publications/aesthetic-design-guidelines)
