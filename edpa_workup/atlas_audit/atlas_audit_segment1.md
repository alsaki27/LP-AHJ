## Coverage and how to reproduce it

| Data group | Reviewed result |
|---|---|
| Alignment, geography and class/issuer summaries | All **19** feature lines and vertices match the original KMZ within **2.23 ft**. Geodesic length **122,976.95 ft** versus **122,981 ft** from the app’s rounded projected feature lengths. **51 measured boundary intervals** sum to **122,980.1 ft** and use District 6. The report also supplies **12 blank/summary rows** tagged Segment 1. All **124 authority runs**, **176 land runs**, **six class**, **15 issuer** and **five collapsed land category** aggregates reconcile to **122,935 ft**. Seventeen sampled feature ends truncate up to 5 ft each, explaining the 46-ft shortfall from the rounded route sum. Totals reconcile internally. Legal boundaries and permit issuance are not derived from those sums. |
| Land and road screening | Reviewed 32 shared parcel report rows, the nine S1 width summaries, 10 S1 DOT gate rows, and road-maintenance rows for Home, Liberty, Sawmill, Watkins, Delaware Ave/US 36. | Crossings and risk rows | All **11** S1 primary risks (R004, R005, R016–R019, R025, R028, R029, R034, R035) and **16** S1 supplemental leads (L008–L023) accounted for above.
