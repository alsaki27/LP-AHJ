# Chicago spurs: public utility source audit

**13 November 2026.** Focused review of official sources for Chicago Spurs 1–4. No proposed route geometry was submitted to external GIS. This pass did not find a complete publicly downloadable Village/County line-level water, sanitary, storm, signal or lighting utility atlas. Existing MWRD public sewer layers and five official roadway/project PDFs remain the spatial screening evidence; neither proves all utilities or depth.

The listed `PublicWorks/SSewerALLSYSview` MapServer returned 404 on current request, so it was not used as evidence. | Request the county storm atlas extract and source-owner attribution for the Spur1 DuPage transition from **DuPage Stormwater Management**, plus actual owner's as-builts. | MWRD sewer facilities near Spur1/2 | Previously acquired [MWRD public sewer layers](https://gispub.mwrd.org/arcgis/rest/services/IncidentBoundary/MapServer) gave 51 route-proximity leads, with three approximate line intersections in `MWRD_public_sewer_route_screen.csv`. The public geometries do not establish ownership at every local pipe or actual invert/depth. | MWRD plan/profile and current asset status for those three screened crossings; reconcile Village vs MWRD ownership before design. |

**Still unverified across Spurs 1–4:** municipal and private water mains/services; sanitary mains and laterals; local storm pipes, inlets, culverts and outfalls; IDOT/Cook/municipal signals, lighting, ITS and conduit; gas distribution/transmission; electric transmission/distribution; telephone, cable and other fiber; inactive/abandoned lines and encasements. Public project PDFs and GIS must be followed by owner records and field utility designation. No jurisdiction or utility owner was contacted in this pass.
