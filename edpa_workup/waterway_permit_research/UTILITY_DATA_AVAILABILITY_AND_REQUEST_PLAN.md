## Overlay files in this workspace

- `Union_County_public_sewer_route_screen.csv` and `MWRD_public_sewer_route_screen.csv` contain source feature IDs, approximate source-feature station, minimum GIS offset and intersection flag.
- `ROW_and_public_utility_feasibility_overlay.gpkg` is a GIS-ready package with 52 original route lines, 1,362 station bands carrying parcel/ROW leads, all 5,061 Ohio/Illinois corridor parcel polygons, 282 Franklin, 29 Cook and two DuPage mapped ROW polygons, 130 route/asset event points, 71 public sewer line geometries, and 59 public sewer structure points. It is a screening overlay, not surveyed utility or legal ROW geometry.
- `public_utility_sources/` retains the downloaded Union County ZIP/extracted shapefiles and the five MWRD corridor GeoJSON query results.
- `utility_records_request_queue_by_jurisdiction.csv` groups the still-needed line-level and as-built records into 27 route/jurisdiction ranges. County/place boundaries route the inquiry; they do not prove asset ownership. No request was sent.
- `build_union_sewer_screen.py` and `build_mwrd_sewer_screen.py` reproduce the two 250 ft GIS screens. Both project the source route to the dataset's working CRS before measuring distance. The stations are projected approximations from each source KMZ feature start.
