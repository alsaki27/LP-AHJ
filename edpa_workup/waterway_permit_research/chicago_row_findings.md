## Recorder and parcel limits

The [DuPage Recorder](https://recorder.dupagecounty.gov/) says its online indexed images begin **16 March 1961**. Document `507662` is identified on the IDOT highway plat as recorded **September 30, 1946**, so its image likely requires Recorder Research or an archival request; its public page lists Research Phone **630-407-5401**. The official [DuPage search page](https://recorder.dupagecounty.gov/Search.aspx) supports document number, subdivision, PIN, and recorded-plat searches. The original image for `507662` was not obtained here.

**Unit correction:** the older `lp_source_copy/edpa_workup/query_dupage_row_by_feature.py` and `query_chicago_row_evidence.py` multiplied EPSG:3435 geometry lengths by 3.28084 even though that CRS is already in US survey feet. The old DuPage intersection-length values (5,420 ft and 163 ft) were therefore overstated. The station ranges and corrected 1,652 ft/50 ft overlaps above come from fresh official polygon geometry and the source route. The older Cook CSV lengths have the same scaling error and must not be used as reported; its count and document/job fields remain leads.

The local Chicago assessor parcel and direct-intersection CSVs contain PINs and route station intervals, but an assessor polygon or mailing owner is not an ROW deed. The shared `ROW_feasibility_station_overlay.csv` now has station/parcel links, Cook ROW screen IDs and DuPage OIDs at station level. All 888 Cook and 27 DuPage corridor parcel polygons were fetched by source OID and added to `ROW_and_public_utility_feasibility_overlay.gpkg`. To make the ROW screen decisive, retrieve the official Cook polygon identifiers and controlling scans, overlay the legal instrument boundaries, and test the proposed work footprint against them.

1. **Cook:** Obtain the three job files, all source plat/deed references for the 29 intersecting ROW GIS polygons, and corresponding recorded sheets; resolve 1899/9999999999 placeholders rather than treating them as historical dates or document numbers.
2. **DuPage:** Retrieve original 1946 Addison Township Supervisors Assessment Plat No. 15 (`ROW_DOC=507662`); obtain the source for OID 20186; check dedications, vacations, road transfers and revisions at the Cook–DuPage transition.
3. **IDOT/Cook/Villages:** Obtain the controlling highway or village road plans and official road-owner determination at exact route stations, including pits, access and traffic control, for Higgins, Busse, Arlington Heights, Devon, Landmeier and local streets.
4. **Private/rail/creek work areas:** Search recorded easements and property instruments wherever the intended route, bore pits or access leave documented public ROW. The line-centerline parcel overlap alone cannot resolve this.

**Feasibility status:** The public GIS and acquired project plans establish a targeted document queue, not occupancy rights or a final buildable corridor. No agency or recorder was contacted, and no title conclusion is made.

## Actual PDF evidence added

Five validated official PDFs are listed in `Chicago_pdf_evidence_manifest.csv` and stored under `row_pdf_evidence/Chicago/`: Cook County's Landmeier contract/utility-adjustment schedule, IDOT/Cook Landmeier–Busse plan sheets, IDOT's 2026 Higgins Road plans, IDOT's 2026 Busse Road plans, and IDOT/Cook/DuPage's Devon–Arlington Heights Road plans. Each begins with a PDF signature and opens successfully (473, 63, 62, 60 and 79 pages respectively). The Landmeier–Busse plans depict `EX ROW` on numerous sheets; this is a project drawing depiction, not the recorded acquisition or dedication instrument.

The newly retrieved [Devon–Arlington Heights PDF](https://apps.dot.illinois.gov/eplan/desenv/011725/144-61L08/PLANS/PL-61L08-144.pdf) has a stronger ROW source: PDF pp. **24–26** are three official **Plat of Highways** sheets, ROW job **R-55-001-97**. PDF p. 25 identifies **Addison Township Supervisors Assessment Plat No. 15, recorded September 30, 1946 as document 507662**, and depicts existing ROW lines, bearings, State coordinates and a temporary easement acquisition parcel **0001TE**. Its existing ROW recorded-information table lists document numbers **R2008-133922, 10779387, R72-64244, 26939495, R2007-196914, R2008-059985, 0820729060, R2008-131478**. These are document citations within the project plat, not retrieved underlying recorded images. PDF pp. 46–47 show the Devon/Arlington Heights intersection, existing ROW and signal/utility modifications. Route match is the **Spur1-S01-Bore** Devon crossing candidate **1,603–1,803 ft**, overlapping DuPage OID 20226 at **1,652–1,702 ft**; the plan coordinate system and route still need a measured survey fit.

**Exact remaining Devon/Arlington Heights record requests:** Obtain the 1946 document `507662` and the eight instruments named in the PDF p. 25 recorded-information table above from the appropriate Cook/DuPage recording offices. Confirm the current status of the illustrated `0001TE` temporary easement, any later releases or revisions, and whether any recorded plan supersedes the 2024 project plat. The project plat supplies a usable search index and geometric control; it does not substitute for the original title instruments.

**Utility completeness:** None of the five PDFs proves every public utility on the four spurs. The Devon/Arlington Heights plan's PDF p. 2 states public and private utility locations are approximate and not guaranteed by the engineer; PDF p. 46 requires field verification before signal work. Water, sewer, storm, gas, electric, telecom, fiber and district facilities still require owner atlases, current record drawings and field designation/potholing as applicable. The public plan lines are conflict-screening evidence only.

The Village's publicly indexed 40-sheet Nicholas/Touhy/McCabe water-main PDF and 2026 Pratt plan PDF could be read through search but direct downloads from `elkgrove.org/home/showpublisheddocument` returned CDN Access Denied. They are **not** counted as local PDF evidence. Spur 2's Oakton/Stanley municipal plan and all underlying Cook/DuPage recorded ROW instruments remain missing. The Village [2026 Business Park Resurfacing project page](https://www.elkgrove.org/government/public-works-department/village-construction-projects/business-park-resurfacing-program) confirms Stanley Street work from Oakton Street to Gullo Avenue and sewer adjustments, but supplies no downloadable plan sheet, ROW plat or utility as-built for the Spur2 crossing.
