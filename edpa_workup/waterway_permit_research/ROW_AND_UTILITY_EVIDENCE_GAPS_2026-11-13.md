# Route ROW and utility evidence: second-pass gap audit

**13 November 2026.** This audit covers the 52 original KMZ route features in S0, S1, and Chicago Spurs 1–4. The master PDF catalog, exact missing-scan register, and feature-level matrix are supplied alongside this report. A PDF linked to a road or nearby utility is a *candidate*, not proof that the proposed trench, bore pits, vaults, staging, and access are inside public ROW or clear of buried plant.

## Answer

**No.** We do not yet have controlling, surveyed, station-matched ROW evidence for every feature, and we do not have a complete current utility inventory for any full route. The feature matrix therefore marks every trench-side choice **HOLD**. Public parcel/ROW polygons, road plans, and utility GIS support screening only. The missing-scan register counts indexed references or record groups, not every legal instrument in the corridor.

The library has **183 validated PDFs / 2,160 pages**. Only **27 of 52 features** have even a local PDF candidate; none has grade A controlling ROW and work-envelope verification. The indexed missing-scan register has **94 entries/groups**: 33 ODOT plan-index OIDs, 29 Cook mapped-ROW polygons, two DuPage mapped-ROW polygons, eight Chicago instruments cited by IDOT, and 22 S1 cited record groups. The 25 features without a candidate PDF are seven S0, 16 S1, one Spur 1, and one Spur 2. Public utility layers yielded **2,935 asset-to-feature proximity links on 29 of 52 features**; a single asset can link to multiple features. No hit on the other 23 does not prove that they lack utilities.

- **Union S1:** road-record compilations are present, but referenced deeds, survey records, journal entries, and plats need original images and station fitting.

## ROW evidence still missing

1. **S0/S1 state roads:** the exact 33 ODOT OhROW indexed plan/acquisition sheets and revisions identified in `missing_controlling_pdf_register.csv`. A repeat query of every public OID found null plan-sheet, attachment, and recorded-document fields; Plan Vault candidates led to internal file paths, rather than downloadable controlling PDFs. The `ODOT_33_OhROW_retry_findings.md` lists all 33 IDs and corrects a misleading `CRS` route-number interpretation. Confirm each plan with ODOT District 5/6 and fit it to the route.
2. **Chicago Cook ROW:** source plat/deed/plan for the mapped ROW polygons with placeholder `ROWDOC_NO`, including the Cook `JOB_NO` leads. Request from Cook DoTH and the recording office, and verify road jurisdiction.
3. **Chicago DuPage ROW:** original `ROW_DOC=507662` scan, the eight instruments on IDOT 61L08 page 25, and any later amendments. The IDOT plat is a locator, not the complete chain of title.
4. **S1 Union/Delaware/municipal roads:** underlying deed, plat, survey, journal, dedication and vacation images cited by public road records. Check current ownership/maintenance and station limits.
5. **Every feature:** surveyed present ROW boundary and any easement limits overlaid with the *whole* proposed construction footprint, including bore entry/exit pits, vaults, handholes, temporary occupation, and access. A nearby recorded strip does not prove this fit.

The exact indexed references and retrieval status are in `row_pdf_evidence/missing_controlling_pdf_register.csv`; the source-road crosswalk is in `route_evidence_gap_matrix.csv`.

## Requests needed to close the utility gap

For each jurisdiction and utility owner, request current GIS/CAD and stamped plan/profile/as-built sheets for water and services, sanitary and laterals, storm/drainage/culverts, gas, electric, communications/fiber, traffic signals, lighting/ITS, abandoned/relocated facilities, and easements. Include route station limits and the planned trench/bore/vault/access envelope, then reconcile dates and owners. Obtain [OHIO811 design-stage responses](https://oups.org/idig/) in Ohio and [JULIE planning/design responses](https://www.illinois1call.com/planning-design/) in Illinois. Private and service lines require separate checks where the public locate system or owner records do not cover them. At candidate conflict points, use field utility designation and test holes/SUE for reliable horizontal and vertical clearance. No design ticket, records request, or owner contact has been submitted in this audit.

The existing `utility_records_request_queue_by_jurisdiction.csv` routes the requests to 27 county/place combinations. The new feature matrix identifies where public screening data exists. Neither is an owner signoff or a permit.
