## 3. Universal utility drawing standard for this bid

Use one authoritative utility base with these fields per feature/station: utility owner; type/material/size if known; horizontal alignment; depth/elevation if supported; location quality (record, surface, SUE QL-D/C/B/A); source and retrieval date; field date; crossing/conflict; proposed fiber/casing/profile; cover and separation; bore pits/workspace; ROW/edge-of-pavement offsets; storm/drainage, structures and water/rail; mitigation; owner response and adjustment duration. Use symbology distinguishing record-only from surveyed/potholed locations. Do not imply record data is field-verified.

For design, request all incumbent as-builts and utility-owner atlases before final route profile; submit Ohio utility design notices / Illinois JULIE design notices; survey visible facilities; use QL-B planning in congested corridors and QL-A at critical conflicts as a vendor recommendation; set potholes around freeway crossings, bridge ends, shallow utility crossings and all planned pits. Reconcile every permit agency’s own checklist at intake. A construction locate ticket is not a substitute for design-quality information.

## 4. Vendor schedule and workflow

### Pre-bid / due-diligence sequence

1. **Freeze bid basis**: confirm endpoint/tie-in at each Chicago spur; correct Marysville/New Albany direction; repair the ~31.1m Ohio segment join gap; clarify installed-LF billing basis and whether the four spur prices and Ohio S0/S1 prices are separate line items.
2. **Station route to controlling road**: split each feature at road, municipal, county, township, state and private-property boundaries. Use the Station Ledger to screen; produce an owner-reconciled station map. Obtain road acceptance/ownership record, acquisition plan/deed, ROW width, dedication/easement and any maintenance agreement. Written-confirm issuer per road segment.
3. **ODOT pilot**: advance D6 candidate bands above, especially I-270/I-71, US23, SR315 and US36/US33. Pull OhROW ROW plans and current project plans. Confirm District utility vs City permit vs shared concurrence in writing; confirm portal checklist, access-control rules, fees, insurance and permit segmentation.
4. **Local intake**: send pre-application packages to Columbus, New Albany, Marysville, Powell, Worthington, Delaware/Union/Franklin/Licking Engineer and applicable township issuers. Ask one standardized set: permittee, exact ROW, acceptable route/method, utility drawing/quality level, fee/bond, review SLA and resubmittal clock, moratorium/current projects, validity/notice/inspection requirements.
5. **Property and crossings**: retrieve railroad title/track/ROW plans and identify license signatory; get water/bridge owner and construction limits; confirm route/property rights for Columbus water-supply parcels. Compare HDD, existing casing/bridge, route shift, aerial or other alternatives per owner acceptance—not assumed feasibility.
6. **Utility base / SUE**: initiate Ohio statutory utility design requests and JULIE planning notices, request municipal/county as-builts, compile quality-coded utility map; scope survey/geotech/test holes at conflicts. Keep utility adjustment lead time separate from permit review time.
7. **Constraints overlay**: collect pavement moratoria, five-year streets, resurfacing/CIP, road closures, access-control, seasonal limits, rail flagging, water/floodplain and ecological work windows. Confirm applicable dates with current road owner.
8. **Price and schedule**: quote engineering per requested segment, feasibility study separately, permit production/coordination, survey/SUE/pothole allowances, railroad/water packages, utility-owner adjustments, bonds/fees/reimbursables and explicit review/resubmittal cycles. Do not turn a planning allowance into an SLA or a guaranteed construction date.

### Permit-ready submittal gates

A segment cannot move to “permit-ready” until (a) controlling authority and ROW limit are confirmed; (b) utility owner/applicant authority/franchise is confirmed; (c) route/pits/access are inside rights; (d) agency drawing checklist is reconciled; (e) known existing utilities and quality/source are shown; (f) MOT/restoration, cover/separation and environmental constraints are resolved; (g) current fee/bond/insurance and review sequence are priced; (h) railroad/water/property approvals are separate and sequenced; (i) permit validity, prestart notice, inspections and closeout are assigned.

## 6. Evidence status and what this tracker does not claim

- District and place overlays describe geography; road-centerline and parcel intersections are screening leads. They do not establish owned ROW, a legal easement, or permit responsibility.
- The exact ODOT ROW boundary, state access-control line, road owner, railroad fee owner/license signatory, waterway jurisdiction and private property right remain to be confirmed from source plans/instruments and written owner responses.
- Review clocks are labeled as SLA, target, filing lead, notice or planning allowance. Unknown is explicitly not treated as zero.
- The route is conceptual; source `Bore/Water/HW/RR` labels are unverified. S0/S1 join gap, reversed route direction and endpoint ties must be settled before final geometry or fixed LF.
- Use `AHJ_Permit_Readiness_Tracker.xlsx` for filterable station-level data, authority rules, Ohio/Chicago road gates, water crossings and rail intersections. The underlying CSVs, deep reports and source manifest remain in this package.
