## Method plan and feasibility study

### Base engineering approach

- Underground fiber in documented existing public road/utility corridors where the actual owner’s occupancy rights, franchise/license and permit conditions allow it. Do not assume corridor use from proximity to a road or parcel polygon.
- HDD or other accepted trenchless method at confirmed streams, railroad and hard-surface roads, subject to geotechnical design, cover/clearance and permit. Open trench only where surface, utilities and owner restoration rules allow.
- Exclude bridge attachment and longitudinal limited-access occupancy from the base method until written approval; compare local street alternatives if the route would require either.
